# Changelog

## 1.2.0 - 2026-11-25
**The big one.**
- **Background playback**: music keeps going with the screen off or the app closed. Notification and lock-screen controls, headphone and Bluetooth buttons, pauses for calls and when headphones are unplugged.
- **New theme: iTunes 2011**: glossy iPhone OS 4 look with a Songs / Artists / Albums switcher (replaces Classic Mac).
- **Secret theme, new unlock**: play any Undertale or Deltarune song to unlock it. It now uses the bundled *Determination* font.
- **FIGHT mode**: the song attacks you on its beats (real beat detection). Dodge with your SOUL, hit FIGHT on the beat for combos, use ACT / ITEM / MERCY.
- Search, albums, favorites, recently added and most played, in every theme.
- Play Next, Add to Queue, Up Next list.
- Real shuffle: every song once before any repeats.
- Sleep timer with fade-out (or stop at the end of the song), equalizer presets, playback speed.
- Remembers your queue, song and position between launches.
- Smooth fling scrolling in all lists.
- Android 13/14 ready (new media permission, notification permission, target SDK 34).
